SEO

What "optimised for Google" actually means

Plenty of people claim to do it. Since you have no way to check, here is exactly what is included in your website — with no terms you have to go and look up.

  • The site loads fast A slow site loses visitors before it even appears. Yours is built light, with no unnecessary weight.
  • Google understands every page Each page tells Google what it is about, with titles and descriptions written around what people actually search for.
  • Works well on mobile Most people will find you on a phone. Google knows that and favours sites that work well there.
  • Google finds everything on its own The site is delivered with the page map Google uses to crawl and index everything you have.
  • Shows up on Google Maps I set up your Google My Business, which is what makes your business show on the map and in searches by people nearby.

Showing up on Google is like building a reputation in a new country

At first nobody knows who you are. Your name means nothing to anyone, and trust is earned with time and presence. The same happens with Google: a new website needs time to be taken seriously, however well it's built.

That's the work that continues after delivery, and it's a service separate from the website. It only makes sense when you want to take that step — we talk when the time comes.

    FAQ

    What people usually ask me

    What do you need from me during those 14 days?

    Little, and all at the start: half an hour of conversation, the texts or the ideas of what you want to say, a few photos and the logo, if you have one.

    If you have no texts ready and no photos, say so. That stops nothing — we sort it out together in the call.

    Do I need to pay for hosting? Is it included in the price?

    Every website needs hosting and a domain — the site address, for example www.oseunegocio.nl — to stay online. That's not included in the website price, and you have two ways to handle it.

    I take care of everything for €19.90 a month, excl. VAT. That amount covers hosting, the domain and website maintenance. You don't need to know anything about technology or sort anything out in Dutch.

    Or you arrange it yourself, directly with the provider, and it all stays in your name. In that case too I stay alongside you, helping you choose and set it up, at no extra cost.

    You choose whichever you prefer, and you know the amounts before deciding. No surprises on the invoice.

    What if I want to change something after the 2 revisions?

    After the 2 included revisions, any change is separate work. It goes like this: you tell me what you need, I tell you what it costs before starting, and it only goes ahead if you agree.

    There's no contract and no lock-in. The monthly maintenance is optional and, if one day you want to stop, you stop.

    Is the website in my name? Am I the owner?

    Yes. The website is yours: the content, the text, the images and the page design belong to your business.

    If you arrange hosting and the domain yourself, they are registered in your name from the start, and I stay alongside you through the whole process.

    What is Google My Business and what is it for?

    It's your business listing on Google. It's what makes a panel appear — with your name, opening hours, phone number and location on the map — when someone searches for your service in your area.

    For anyone working with customers in their region, it's usually what brings the most enquiries. It comes set up with your website at no extra cost.

    Which part of the Netherlands do you work in? And in which languages?

    I'm based in Enschede, but I work remotely with the whole country. Being in the same city was never needed to build a website: we arrange everything by call.

    I work in Portuguese and English. The whole process — quote, questions, revisions, delivery — runs in one of those languages.

    Shall we talk about your website?

    Half an hour, in your language, with no obligation. You tell me what you do, I tell you frankly whether I can help and what it costs. If it doesn't make sense, I say that too.
